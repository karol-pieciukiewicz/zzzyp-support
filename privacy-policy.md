# Privacy Policy

**App:** Zzzyp - Baby White Noise
**Last updated:** 18 October 2026

## Short version

Zzzyp collects nothing. It has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers. It does not connect to the internet.

## What data is collected

**None.**

We do not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal data, usage data, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, location, contacts, photos, microphone audio, or diagnostics. The app's App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected".

## What is stored on your device

Zzzyp saves a few small values locally on your phone so the app remembers them between launches:

- your chosen fade-out duration
- your chosen quick add-minutes value
- whether cry detection is on, and how long the sound keeps playing after crying stops
- whether you have already seen the welcome screen
- how many times you have started a sound, used only to decide when to show Apple's standard "rate this app" prompt once

These never leave your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

## Microphone and cry detection

Cry detection is optional and off by default. You turn it on from the main screen or in Settings. Zzzyp first explains what it does, and only if you agree does your phone ask for microphone access.

While cry detection is on, the microphone listens for a baby crying. When it hears crying, your chosen sound starts, and it fades out after the time you picked once the crying stops. The audio is analysed on your phone, as it happens, by Apple's built-in sound classifier, and then discarded. Nothing is recorded, saved, or sent anywhere, and no person or company ever hears it. Your phone's microphone indicator stays on while cry detection is on. Turn cry detection off and the microphone stops. You can also withdraw microphone access at any time in your phone's Settings.

Cry detection is not a baby monitor or a safety device. It sends no alerts and it can miss crying.

## Network access

The app makes no network requests. It works fully offline. All eight sounds are bundled inside the app; nothing is downloaded or streamed.

## Third parties

Zzzyp contains no advertising networks, no analytics providers, no crash-reporting services, and no social media SDKs. No third party receives any information about you through this app.

## Children

Zzzyp is designed to be used by parents and other adults, including while caring for an infant. Because the app collects no data whatsoever, it collects no data from children either.

## Permissions

Zzzyp asks for one permission: the microphone, and only when you turn on cry
detection (see above). It does not ask for notifications, location, contacts,
photos, or tracking. It plays audio in the background, which on iOS needs no
permission prompt.

## Health disclaimer

Zzzyp plays ambient sound. It is not a medical device and makes no claim to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. If you have concerns about your sleep or your child's sleep, speak to a healthcare professional. As with any audio played near an infant, keep the volume low and the device out of the cot.
