# Privacy Policy

**App:** Zzzyp - Baby White Noises
**Last updated:** 22 August 2026

## Short version

Zzzyp collects nothing. It has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers. It does not connect to the internet.

## What data is collected

**None.**

We do not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal data, usage data, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, location, contacts, photos, microphone input, or diagnostics.

## What is stored on your device

Zzzyp saves three small preferences locally on your phone so the app remembers them between launches:

- your chosen fade-out duration
- your chosen quick add-minutes value
- whether you have already seen the welcome screen

These never leave your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

## Network access

The app makes no network requests. It works fully offline. All eight sounds are bundled inside the app; nothing is downloaded or streamed.

## Third parties

Zzzyp contains no advertising networks, no analytics providers, no crash-reporting services, and no social media SDKs. No third party receives any information about you through this app.

## Children

Zzzyp is designed to be used by parents and other adults, including while caring for an infant. Because the app collects no data whatsoever, it collects no data from children either. It complies with COPPA and the GDPR by having nothing to comply about.

## Permissions

Zzzyp requests no permissions. It does not ask for notifications, microphone,
location, contacts, photos, or tracking. It plays audio in the background,
which on iOS needs no permission prompt.

## Health disclaimer

Zzzyp plays ambient sound. It is not a medical device and makes no claim to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. If you have concerns about your sleep or your child's sleep, speak to a healthcare professional. As with any audio played near an infant, keep the volume low and the device out of the cot.
