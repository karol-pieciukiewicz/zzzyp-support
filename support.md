# Zzzyp — Support

**Zzzyp - Baby White Noise** — eight sleep sounds, a fade-out timer, background playback, and cry detection. All free, no ads, no accounts.

## Getting help

Open an issue: <https://github.com/karol-pieciukiewicz/zzzyp-support/issues>

Please include your device model, OS version, and the app version (shown at the bottom of the Settings screen).

## Common questions

**Is anything paid?**
No. Every sound and every feature is free. There are no ads, no subscriptions and no in-app purchases.

**Does the sound keep playing when I lock the phone?**
Yes. That is the point of the app. Start a sound and lock the screen — playback continues and you get lock-screen controls. If it stops, check that Low Power Mode or a battery optimiser is not killing background audio.

**Can I control it without unlocking the phone?**
Yes. While a sound is playing, standard play and pause controls appear on the lock screen and in Control Centre.

**How do I stop the sound after a while?**
Tap a timer chip on the main screen, or open the timer for a custom length or a stop-at clock time. The sound fades out gradually at the end instead of cutting off. Fade length is adjustable in Settings.

**What is cry detection?**
Turn on "Cry detection" on the main screen. Zzzyp then listens for your baby crying without playing anything. When crying starts, your chosen sound plays, keeps going for a while after the crying stops (set this in Settings), then fades out and listens again. It works with the screen locked. Tap it again to turn it off, or use Settings.

**Does cry detection record my baby?**
No. The sound is analysed on your phone as it happens and then discarded. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent. The microphone indicator on your phone stays on while cry detection is on.

**Cry detection did not start the sound.**
Check that microphone access is allowed for Zzzyp in your phone's Settings, and that the phone is in the same room and not covered. Cry detection is not a baby monitor and can miss crying, so do not rely on it for safety.

**Does it use data or work on a plane?**
It works fully offline and never uses data. All sounds are bundled inside the app.

**Does it collect any data?**
No. See the [Privacy Policy](https://karol-pieciukiewicz.github.io/zzzyp-support/privacy-policy/).
